Where each number came from

Requests received (69, 60, 69)Counted every request received Mon Sept 21 to Fri Sept 25, did the same for the week before, and averaged the 4 weeks before this one.
Finished on time (86.4%, 71.2%)Took every request finished this week and checked each one against the deadline recorded on that request. Same method for last week.
Avg turnaround (4.36 days)Averaged the business days from received to finished for every request completed this week.
Harborline on time (71%, 53%, 78%)Counted every Harborline request finished this week (21) and September to date (88) and checked each against its own deadline. The 78% is the average of March to May; the 6-month comparison uses full months only.
Harborline 3-day subpoenasSplit this week's finished Harborline requests by the deadline written on each one: 18 on a 5-day clock, 3 subpoenas on a 3-day clock.
Harborline 4.8 vs 6.5 daysAveraged business days, then calendar days, from received to finished for the same 21 requests, to show why a leader's figure might differ.
Beacon trend (-58%)Counted Beacon requests received per month for the last 6 full months and compared the average of the last 3 months with the 3 before.
Keystone volume (17 vs 7.8)Counted Keystone Auto Group requests received this week and compared them to its 4-week average.
Backlog (56 and 953)Counted requests currently received, in review or on hold, and separately those waiting on payment.
Unpaid invoices ($75,239; RecordPoint $26,467)Added up every invoice still marked open, then grouped by requestor. Invoices over 90 days are measured from the invoice date to Fri Sept 25.
RecordPoint portal tickets (22)Counted RecordPoint's support tickets tagged as portal login since March 1.
Support tickets (3, 2, 6.5)Counted tickets opened this week, last week, and averaged the 4 weeks before this one, then grouped this week's by category.
