**Top 5 stricter than standard (6 more in the workbook)**
1. **Turnaround is 5 business days, subpoenas 3** (Amend. 2 s.1 and s.2, replacing 7 and 5). Amend. 2 s.3 adds a 2% service credit for any month where more than 5% of requests miss 5 days. In the sample request log, non-subpoena misses ran 9% (Apr), 7% (May), 28% (Jul), 41% (Aug) and 30% (Sep to date). By that count the credit applied in at least 5 of the last 6 months.
2. **Incident notice within 24 hours of discovery, suspected incidents included** (MSA s.4.2). The standard is 72 hours from confirmation.
3. **Written approval from Harborline's claim representative before any release to an adverse carrier** (MSA s.3.2). The standard needs no approval.
4. **Withhold any page that references mental health treatment** unless the authorization names mental health records (Amend. 2 s.4, new MSA s.3.4). This covers far more than the standard's psychotherapy-notes rule.
5. **Working copies deleted within 14 days of delivery**, not 30 (MSA s.6.1).

The other 6: the AI approval (s.4.4), continental-US-only data (s.4.3), the weekly Monday service-level report, whole-page withholding of SIU/reserve/valuation pages, DL/plate/VIN redaction, and audits on 10 business days' notice.
